diagnostics and source manifests. This repository imports a new immutable bundle
and renders its existing values. Stage31c pooled CON/SIS results and earlier
descriptive figures remain supporting analyses; they are not overwritten.

The Stage31d protocol documents the revision from automatic boundary-variance
withholding to numerical/refit acceptance with audited boundary flags. It retains
the full animal/cage covariance structure, four overall tests, twelve constrained
pairwise tests, and independent multiplicity families. Pointwise intervals are
not simultaneous confidence intervals. The plan was revised after descriptive
and condition-result inspection and remains exploratory.

The figure checks the exact animal, group, batch and cage identities within each
sex/phase in both source bundles; matching counts alone cannot pass that check.
