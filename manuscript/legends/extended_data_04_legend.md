# Extended Data Figure 4 legend

**Extended Data Figure 4 | Early behaviour and later integrated resilience: secondary features, the complete registered prediction model set, and sex.**

**(a)** Associations of the two registered secondary early features, movement RMSSD and entropy ACF1, with the later continuous composite outcome, integrated resilience (CombZ). Points and intervals show the stored association estimates and their uncertainty; the printed adjusted values use the registered association family. These estimates accompany the primary mean-movement result and do not provide independent replication.

**(b)** All five registered models, displayed in registry order: an intercept-only baseline, mean movement, the three-feature behavioural model (mean movement, movement RMSSD and entropy ACF1), and the corresponding two models with sex added. The same 111 animals enter the prediction analysis. Points show leave-one-animal-out R². Bars show the 2.5th–97.5th percentile range across repeated grouped five-fold splits, with the repeated-cross-validation mean indicated separately; these bars are not confidence intervals for the leave-one-animal-out points. Animal identity defines the held-out unit. Where a full-refit outcome-permutation test was registered, the stored raw permutation P value is shown; Figure 1 reports the Holm-adjusted value across the two behaviour-only models; models without a registered permutation test are labelled accordingly. Display order does not select a model or constitute a test of differences between models. Validation is internal.

**(c)** Formal feature-by-sex interaction estimates for the three registered early features, with their stored 95% confidence intervals and Benjamini–Hochberg adjusted values across the three interaction tests. These interaction tests address whether the feature–outcome relationships differ between sexes. A detectable association within one sex alone does not establish a sex difference, and an interval including zero does not demonstrate equivalence.

**(d)** Sex-stratified Spearman correlations for the same three early features, shown as descriptive context for the formal interaction analysis. The sample comprises 58 females and 53 males. Sex is nested in experimental batch, which limits biological attribution of differences between sexes.

The early behavioural window is the first active phase after the first cage change (CC1), a 12-hour window represented by 72 ten-minute target slots per animal. Coverage refers to the target-slot representation constructed under the registered carry-forward position rule. All 111 animals have all 72 target slots (7,992 in total), with no missing leading, interior or trailing slots; mean and minimum coverage are 100%.

Sources: the corrected-outcome behaviour bundle ebb_v102_20261019_677ed97 (C2 estimates and E multiplicity) and figure-support bundle fsb_v5_20261019_8212df4 (E1 sex-stratified correlations, E3 early-window coverage). Numerical results are copied from the frozen upstream analysis; this renderer computes no association or significance test. The corrected outcome is rerun under configuration v1.0.2 with the original analytic definitions retained. The primary movement-only prediction uses uncentred measurements and outcomes.
