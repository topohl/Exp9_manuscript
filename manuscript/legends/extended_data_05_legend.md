# Extended Data Figure 5 legend

**Extended Data Figure 5 | Coverage of the early behavioural window and the complete registered prediction model set.**

**(a)** Coverage of the first active phase after the first cage change (CC1), a 12-hour window represented by 72 ten-minute target slots per animal. Coverage refers to the target-slot representation constructed under the registered carry-forward position rule. All 111 animals have all 72 target slots (7,992 in total), with no missing leading, interior or trailing slots; mean and minimum coverage are 100%.

**(b)** All five registered models, displayed in registry order: an intercept-only baseline, mean movement, the three-feature behavioural model (mean movement, movement RMSSD and entropy ACF1), and the corresponding two models with sex added. The same 111 animals enter the prediction analysis. Filled points show leave-one-animal-out R². Bars show the 2.5th–97.5th percentile range across repeated grouped five-fold splits, with the repeated-cross-validation mean indicated separately; these bars are not confidence intervals for the leave-one-animal-out points. Animal identity defines the held-out unit. Where a full-refit outcome-permutation test was registered, the stored raw permutation P value is shown; Figure 1 reports the Holm-adjusted value across the two behaviour-only models; models without a registered permutation test are labelled accordingly. Display order does not select a model or constitute a test of differences between models. Validation is internal.

Sources: the corrected-outcome behaviour bundle ebb_v102_20261019_677ed97 (C2 estimates and E multiplicity) and figure-support bundle fsb_v5_20261019_8212df4 (E3 early-window coverage).
