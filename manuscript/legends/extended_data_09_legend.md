# Extended Data Figure 9 legend

**Extended Data Figure 9 | Secondary behavioural associations and formal tests of feature-by-sex interaction.**

**(a)** Associations of the two registered secondary early features, movement RMSSD and entropy ACF1, with the later continuous composite outcome, CombZ. Points and intervals show the stored association estimates and their uncertainty; the printed adjusted values use the registered association family. These estimates accompany the primary mean-movement result and do not provide independent replication.

**(b)** Formal feature-by-sex interaction estimates for the three registered early features, with their stored 95% confidence intervals and Benjamini–Hochberg adjusted values across the three interaction tests. These interaction tests address whether the feature–outcome relationships differ between sexes. A detectable association within one sex alone does not establish a sex difference, and an interval including zero does not demonstrate equivalence.

**(c)** Sex-stratified Spearman correlations for the same three early features, shown as descriptive context for the formal interaction analysis. The sample comprises 58 females and 53 males. Sex is nested in experimental batch, which limits biological attribution of differences between sexes.

Sources: corrected-outcome behaviour bundle ebb_v102_20261019_677ed97 (C2 estimates and E multiplicity) and figure-support bundle fsb_v5_20261019_8212df4 (E1 sex-stratified correlations). All numerical results are computed upstream and imported with hashes. This renderer computes no association or significance test.
