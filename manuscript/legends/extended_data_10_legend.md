**Extended Data Fig. 10 | Conventional behaviour tests and locomotion, females and males analysed separately.**
**a**, Main readout of each test: elevated plus maze, share of time in the open arms; open field, share of time in the centre; novel object recognition, novel-object preference D2; social preference, preference D2 for the mouse over the empty cup (phase 1) and for the new over the familiar mouse (phase 2). Novel-object D2 is one of the six CombZ components (Extended Data Fig. 11d), so a RES–SUS difference in it, and in the other novel-object measures (Supplementary Fig. X), follows partly from how the groups were defined. Dots, individual animals (CON slate blue, RES grey, SUS red), ordered by batch within each group; dark circle and line, batch-adjusted group mean and 95% confidence interval (linear model value ~ batch + group, per sex). No main readout differed between groups after correction (two-tier Benjamini-Hochberg). **b**, Batch-adjusted Cohen's d (group difference / residual SD) of the main readouts and of every measure with at least one comparison at adjusted P < 0.05 (dot); colour limit ±1. SIS, all stress-exposed animals (RES and SUS together). All 87 measures: Supplementary Fig. X. **c**, Locomotion index by group: the distance travelled in each of the five test sessions, z-scored against the controls of the same sex, averaged over the sessions an animal has (at least three). Dots, means and intervals as in a; grey line, z = 0. Exploratory and not corrected: the index was defined after most nominal group-by-sex interactions turned out to be locomotion measures. No group difference reaches P < 0.05: females, SUS − CON d = 0.59, P = 0.117, RES − SUS d = −0.49, P = 0.133; males, RES − CON d = 0.70, P = 0.051, RES − SUS d = 0.53, P = 0.101; group-by-sex interaction F(2, 107) = 2.48, P = 0.089. **d**, Cohen's d of the distance travelled in each session and of the index; colour as in b. Females, 12 CON, 26 RES, 20 SUS; males, 12 CON, 28 RES, 19 SUS. Fewer in the open field (4 females: 2 CON, 1 RES, 1 SUS), the social preference test (1 female CON) and the object measures of the novel object test (1 male SUS, whose novel object was not tracked; his distance travelled in that test is complete, so c and d include all 19 male SUS).
