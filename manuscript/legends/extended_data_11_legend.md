**Extended Data Fig. 11 | Physiology and the spread of the outcome, females and males analysed separately.**
**a**, Main physiology readouts: weight gain from the first to the last weighing; adrenal and spleen weight per 100 g body weight; corticosterone rise (response minus baseline); sucrose preference over all tests. Adrenal, spleen, corticosterone rise and sucrose preference are components of the composite outcome (CombZ). The weight gain shown here (weighing 1 to 9) is not, but it comes from the same series of weighings as the CombZ weight-gain component (weighing 2 to 6, 3 to 6 in batch 6), and its last weighing is the body weight by which the organ weights are divided. Differences of RES and SUS from each other and from CON in these measures therefore follow, wholly or partly, from how the groups were defined (SIS against CON does not). Dots, means and intervals as in Extended Data Fig. 10a; brackets, pairs with adjusted P < 0.05 (two-tier Benjamini-Hochberg), labelled with the adjusted P. Damaged or incomplete organs (22 measurements of 9 animals, dissection notes) are excluded and 2 further males have no adrenal weight; these organs have no CombZ component either. **b**, Batch-adjusted Cohen's d of the main readouts and of every physiology measure with at least one comparison at adjusted P < 0.05 (dot), as in Extended Data Fig. 10b. The added rows (last weighing, growth rate, adrenal weight in mg, corticosterone response, sucrose preference in tests 1 and 3) come from the same data as components and carry the caveat of a. All 19 measures: Supplementary Fig. Y. **c**, CombZ of control (CON) and stress-exposed (SIS) animals; SIS animals coloured by outcome group. Solid line, mean of the controls; dashed lines, one population SD of the controls above and below it (the lower one is the susceptibility cut-off). After removing batch and condition means, the SD of SIS animals was 1.67 times that of controls in females (95% CI 1.28–2.20; Brown-Forsythe P = 0.129) and 1.88 times in males (1.28–3.12; P = 0.028). **d**, SD ratio SIS / CON (log scale) with 95% residual-bootstrap interval for CombZ (top), its six components (middle: novel-object D2, sucrose preference, weight gain from weighing 2 to 6 [3 to 6 in batch 6], corticosterone rise, adrenal and spleen weight per 100 g body weight) and the other four main readouts of the conventional tests (bottom). The Brown-Forsythe P values (Benjamini-Hochberg within each set) and the ratios within cages are in the source data. Group sizes as in Extended Data Fig. 10, except in a and b: adrenal weight, 7 females (2 CON, 2 RES, 3 SUS) and 4 males (1 CON, 1 RES, 2 SUS) fewer; spleen weight, 2 females (1 CON, 1 SUS) fewer; corticosterone, 1 male RES fewer; sucrose preference in test 1, 2 females (1 CON, 1 SUS) and 1 male RES fewer; in test 3, 6 females (2 CON, 4 RES) and 7 males (1 CON, 3 RES, 3 SUS) fewer. In c and d, 12 CON and 46 (females) or 47 (males) SIS animals; the n of each row of d is in the source data.
