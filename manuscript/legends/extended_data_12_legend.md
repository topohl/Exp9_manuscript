**Extended Data Fig. 12 | Automated scoring agrees with manual scoring.**
Automated scores (SLEAP pose estimation, SLEAPanalyzer; the values analysed in Extended Data Fig. 10) against manual scores (BORIS) of the same videos, 20 male animals of batch 1, the only batch whose conventional tests were scored by hand. **a**, Elevated plus maze: time in the open arms, closed arms and centre, and number of nose dips. **b**, Novel object recognition: contact time with the novel and the familiar object, total contact, D2; manual values from the raw BORIS exports. **c**, Social preference: contact time with the mouse and with the empty cup and preference D2 (phase 1); contact time with the new and the familiar mouse and novelty D2 (phase 2). Each dot is one animal; dashed line, identity; r, Pearson correlation. The nose-dip smoothing window and the object-contact detector were chosen on these 20 animals against this manual scoring, so the agreement of nose dips (a) and of b is a calibration fit, not an independent validation; the plus-maze times and the social tests were not tuned. One animal has a novel-object D2 of opposite sign in the two scorings. Open-arm entries are not shown: the manual record counts behaviour bouts in the open arms, not zone entries (their correlation is in the source data). The open field was not scored manually.
