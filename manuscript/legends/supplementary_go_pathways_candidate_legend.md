# Supplementary GO pathway examples (candidate; number unassigned)

**Additional exact-term enrichment examples and their spatial context.**
The first two pages pair four previously unillustrated members of the
seven-family GO program atlas with protein-level views, following Figure 3
h-m. (a) Translation (GO:0006412) in CA1 stratum oriens neuropil.
(b) Chromatin organization (GO:0006325) in CA2 neuronal soma.
(c) Neuron projection development (GO:0031175) in CA2 stratum radiatum
neuropil. (d) Autophagy (GO:0006914) in CA2 stratum radiatum neuropil.
Each running-enrichment curve uses the stored susceptible-versus-resilient
ranked-gene comparison; short ticks mark genes belonging to the exact GO term.
The three tiles beneath each curve give the stored normalised enrichment score
for RES-CON, SUS-CON and SUS-RES in that same spatial unit. A dot marks
an exact-term BH-adjusted GSEA FDR below 0.05. The paired protein view shows
the seven highest absolute-ranked-statistic leading-edge genes for that term,
using stored protein log2 fold changes in the three contrasts. Contrast is
encoded by both colour and shape. The four protein panels share a symmetric
axis; none of their displayed protein/contrast rows has BH FDR below 0.05.

The regional page shows those four exact terms across the ten neuropil
region-layer units, four neuronal-soma regions and four microglia-enriched
ROIs, separately for each contrast. Tile colour is the stored exact-term NES
on one shared scale; dots retain the same term-level FDR meaning. The
microglia-enriched ROI is a measurement context, not a purified cell
population.

The four GO identifiers were fixed to name the remaining atlas families.
For each exact term, the displayed curve's spatial unit is the one with the
smallest FDR-supported SUS-RES BH FDR in the frozen inventory. This is a
result-dependent display selection, not an independent replication or a
test of regional specificity. The three pairwise contrasts are related.
Neither the dots nor the broader theme atlas constitute a theme-level
significance test. Full values, selection rules, curve points and leading-edge
gene memberships accompany this candidate as source data.
